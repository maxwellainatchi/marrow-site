Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

The short version: your health information stays on the Marrow server you run. The developer of Marrow never receives it. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or sent to an outside AI service. This website collects nothing about you.

Who this policy is from

Marrow is developed by Maxwell Ainatchi ("we", "us"), an individual developer. Marrow is a self-hosted application: each copy runs on a server controlled by the person using it. You can reach us at {{CONTACT}}.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics, trackers or advertising. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) for security and operations; we do not use them to identify visitors.

What Marrow collects

When you connect a health system, Marrow reads, with your authorization, the parts of your record you approve. Depending on what the health system shares, that can include:

  • Demographics (name, date of birth, sex, contact details)
  • Conditions, allergies and intolerances
  • Medications, prescriptions and immunizations
  • Lab results, vital signs and other observations
  • Visits, procedures, care team and clinical notes or documents

You can also type records in by hand or upload PDFs. Marrow only reads from health systems. It never writes to, changes or deletes anything in your health system's record.

Where it is stored

Everything Marrow collects is stored on the server where you run Marrow, in its database and file store. It is not stored on any server run by us. Access tokens from your health system are kept on that server, encrypted, and are never sent to your web browser.

How it is used

Your information is used only to show you your own record: to combine sources, show history and trends, remind you about medications and care, and produce reports you choose to print or share. Any AI features run on a local model on your own server. Your record is never sent to a cloud AI service.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information, and we do not use it for advertising or research. We never receive it in the first place. Marrow sends information elsewhere only when you ask it to, for example:

  • Requests to your health system, using the access you granted, to fetch your record.
  • Lookups of public drug and vaccine reference information (such as DailyMed, MedlinePlus and RxNorm), which send a code, not your record.
  • Calendar events or notifications you turn on, which carry private titles rather than clinical detail.
  • Reports or exports you create and choose to share.

Marrow may need to disclose information if required by law, but because we do not hold your information, such a request would go to whoever runs the server.

Security

Marrow is built to keep data on a private server, with access tokens held server-side, a strict content security policy, encrypted backups, and an app passphrase. No system is perfectly secure, and the security of a self-hosted copy also depends on the server it runs on.

Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time from your health system's patient portal (for example, MyChart's linked apps settings), or disconnect the source in Marrow.
  • Export or delete your data from Marrow's settings, or by deleting the server's data.
  • Correct your record in Marrow with notes and overrides. Corrections in your health system's own record must be requested from that health system.

Retention

Your data is kept on your server until you delete it. Disconnecting a source stops new data from being fetched; already-fetched records stay until you remove them.

Children

Marrow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
