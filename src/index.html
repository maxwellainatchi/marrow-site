Personal health record

Your health record, on your own server.

Marrow gathers your records from your patient portal, PDFs and your own notes, keeps every source exactly as it arrived, and builds one record on top that you can trace back line by line.

Reads, never writes

Marrow connects to your patient portal with your permission and only reads. It never changes anything at your doctor's office.

Every source kept

Each record stays exactly as it came in. The combined record is rebuilt on top, so you can always see where a line came from.

Runs where you choose

Marrow runs on a server you control. There is no Marrow cloud, no account with us, and no advertising.

What it keeps track of

  • Medications, with doses taken and reminders
  • Lab results over time, flagged against their reference ranges
  • Vaccines, with series windows from CDC schedules
  • Visits, conditions and allergies, filterable by specialty
  • A visit report you can print and bring to an appointment

Connecting to Epic (MyChart)

Marrow uses the SMART on FHIR standard to read your record from health systems that use Epic. You sign in to MyChart yourself and choose what to share. Marrow's server keeps the access token; it is never sent to your browser, and you can revoke access from MyChart at any time. See the privacy policy and data use page for exactly what is read and where it goes.
